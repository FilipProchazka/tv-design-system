**FULL** · 56 · Photo corner

**Use when**
A photograph that needs to be seen, with a short claim.

**Roles**
- statement: 102px, 3 lines
- lead: one supporting paragraph
- label: mono, tracked caps
- photograph: duotone in Public mode

**Rules**
- Claim in one corner on a scrim, two lines maximum.
- Keep the subject of the photograph out of the corner you use.
- The board is one surface: photograph or colour to all four edges, type on a real scrim.
- Nothing sits in the footer row (y 962) but the reference and the page number.
