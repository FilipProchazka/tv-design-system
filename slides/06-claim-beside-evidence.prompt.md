**BESIDE** · 06 · Claim beside evidence

**Use when**
A claim that has a source behind it.

**Roles**
- claim: 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- body: evidence, 28px (24px min)

**Rules**
- Grade ticks top-right; the citation goes in the footer reference, not in the body.
- State the limit of the claim in the same breath as the claim.
- The claim steps down to 58px in a BESIDE column; the column never widens.
- One idea beside one piece of evidence. Two pieces of evidence is two boards.
