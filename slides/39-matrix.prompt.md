**DIAGRAM** · 39 · Matrix

**Use when**
Two independent variables crossed.

**Roles**
- claim: 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- data: mono, tabular

**Rules**
- Axis titles in mono at both ends; items are labelled dots, never floating text.
- Four quadrants, and each one is named.
- Nothing thinner than 4px, no node smaller than 26px, no label under 26px.
- If the relationship needs crossing lines, the diagram is wrong: re-draw it as a ring or a chain.
