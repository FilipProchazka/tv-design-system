**BAND** · 58 · Photo caption strip

**Use when**
A picture that introduces three short points.

**Roles**
- claim: 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- body: evidence, 28px (24px min)
- label: mono, tracked caps
- photograph: duotone in Public mode

**Rules**
- Strip height 640px; claim under it, three captions below that.
- Captions are one line each, mono labels above them.
- Photographs in a band share one crop ratio and one duotone. Mixed crops read as a moodboard.
- The sentence under the band says what the pictures have in common.
