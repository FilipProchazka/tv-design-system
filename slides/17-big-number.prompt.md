**BESIDE** · 17 · Big number

**Use when**
One number that carries the point on its own.

**Roles**
- sub: column head, 34px/500
- figure: Geist 300 + mono unit
- body: evidence, 28px (24px min)
- label: mono, tracked caps

**Rules**
- One number per board. A second number is a second board.
- Simplify it: "4 in 10", not 42.7%. The figure is Geist 300, the unit is mono.
- The claim steps down to 58px in a BESIDE column; the column never widens.
- One idea beside one piece of evidence. Two pieces of evidence is two boards.
