**DIAGRAM** · 40 · Concept map

**Use when**
Several things that all relate to each other.

**Roles**
- claim: 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- body: evidence, 28px (24px min)

**Rules**
- Draw it as a ring with a cross behind the discs: never crossing diagonals.
- Claim moves to a BESIDE column so it cannot collide with the graph.
- Nothing thinner than 4px, no node smaller than 26px, no label under 26px.
- If the relationship needs crossing lines, the diagram is wrong: re-draw it as a ring or a chain.
