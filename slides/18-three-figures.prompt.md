**STACK** · 18 · Three figures

**Use when**
Three numbers of the same kind and magnitude.

**Roles**
- claim: 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- figure: Geist 300 + mono unit
- body: evidence, 28px (24px min)
- label: mono, tracked caps

**Rules**
- Same unit, same order of magnitude, same decimal places.
- One sentence of context each, no more.
- Never run three STACK boards in a row: split the argument or draw the relationship.
- Five items maximum in a list, three in a column set. Over that, it is a handout.
