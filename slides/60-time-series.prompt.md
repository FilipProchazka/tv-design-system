**PLOT** · 60 · Time series

**Use when**
A quantity over time with one interesting window.

**Roles**
- claim: 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- data: mono, tabular

**Rules**
- One highlighted window, labelled; the rest is context.
- No second axis. A second quantity is a second board.
- No gridlines, no legend box: label the series at the end of the line or on the bar.
- Axis labels, units and values are mono with tabular numerals. Never red against green.
