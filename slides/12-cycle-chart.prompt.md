**PLOT** · 12 · Cycle chart

**Use when**
Two quantities moving over one shared axis.

**Roles**
- claim: 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- data: mono, tabular

**Rules**
- Label each curve at its end. No legend.
- Sand is the only second data colour; never the topic colour.
- No gridlines, no legend box: label the series at the end of the line or on the bar.
- Axis labels, units and values are mono with tabular numerals. Never red against green.
