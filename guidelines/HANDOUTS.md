# Handouts

A handout is what the room takes home: the takeaways and the tools of a talk, not a print of its slides. Every talk that has one ships two, from the same content: **print** (`templates/handout-zenske-zdravi/`) and **online** (`templates/handout-zenske-zdravi-online/`), which the deck's closing board links by QR.

## Content

- The claims a listener will act on, the practical tools (portion hands, a recipe, a checklist), and what to ask a doctor. Mechanism and backup material stay in the deck.
- Every number carries its source; a short sources list closes the document.
- One sentence says it is educational and does not replace an examination.
- Her affiliations appear once, at the end.

## Setting

- Same topic theme as the deck. Built on `doc-page` (A4 or letter), so it prints and exports to PDF without extra work.
- Text at 16px or larger (12pt); mono labels at 14px or larger.
- Structure is hairline rows, never cards; no radius, no shadow. A table header may carry the band tint.
- Photographs follow the deck's imagery mode and the same library files.
- Czech typography rules as on slides (`TYPESETTING.md`); no em dashes.
