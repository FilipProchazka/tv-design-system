One of the eight subjects, on its own theme tint, with its topic mark.

```jsx
<TopicCard slug="zenske-zdravi" name="Ženské zdraví" theme="violet"
  summary="Co téma pokrývá. A co je v něm sporné." href="/temata/zenske-zdravi" />
```

`slug` doubles as the Icon name, so the mark and the route cannot drift apart. `summary` is two sentences: what the subject covers, and what is contested in it. `theme` is topic colour — blue for clinical, petrol for longevity, violet for women's health — and petrol and violet never become site chrome. The subject's name always sits in text beside the mark.
