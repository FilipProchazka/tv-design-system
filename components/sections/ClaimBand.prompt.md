One sentence over a duotoned photograph, on a real scrim.

```jsx
<ClaimBand claim="Jedna krátká věta." image="/img/…jpg" alt="Co je na snímku" />
```

`claim` is one short sentence, never a paragraph. The scrim is a solid background colour (`rgba(15,53,87,.50)`) plus a bottom gradient: a gradient alone computes as a transparent background and under-measures on contrast tools even when it looks legible. `image` is already duotoned to the site blue; never the topic colour.
