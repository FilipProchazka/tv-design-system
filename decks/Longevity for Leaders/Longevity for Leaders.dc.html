Sleep
Six hours feels fine. Your performance says otherwise.
6 h × 14 nights
built deficits equal to up to two nights without sleep. Participants rated themselves only slightly sleepy.
Aim for a 7–9 hour sleep window and a fixed wake time.

10
A leader's calendar
Travel, caffeine and long days follow physiology, not willpower.
Jet lag
B
The body clock shifts about one hour a day, eastward slower. Seek morning light at the destination; melatonin at local bedtime helps.
Caffeine
A
400 mg taken six hours before bed still cut sleep by more than an hour. Keep it to the morning.
Decision fatigue
C
“Willpower runs out” failed a 23-lab replication. Sleep loss reliably impairs judgement. Rest before big calls.
Herxheimer & Petrie, Cochrane 2002 · Drake et al., J Clin Sleep Med 2013 · Hagger et al., Perspect Psychol Sci 2016

Women's health at work
Menopause arrives in peak career years, and it can be treated.
BSurveys and guidelines
51
median age at natural menopause, often in the most senior years of a career
80%

14
Verdicts · Recovery and tracking
Pleasant, sometimes useful, rarely what the marketing claims.
Sauna
4–7 sessions a week linked to about 40% lower mortality in Finnish men. Observational only. A complement to training, not a substitute.
C
Cold plunge
Short-term drop in stress, possibly better sleep. Straight after strength training it can blunt muscle gain.
C
Wearables
Useful for your own trends in sleep timing and HRV. Weak at sleep stages. One night means little.
P
Glucose monitor
Without diabetes, no outcome data. Normal rises after meals are easy to over-read.
C
Laukkanen et al., JAMA Intern Med 2015 · Cain et al., PLOS One 2025 · Roberts et al., J Physiol 2015
15
Verdicts · What you swallow
One supplement earns its place. Most do not in healthy adults.
Intermittent fasting
In trials no better than eating less. Use it if it helps you do that.
B
Creatine
With strength training, more strength and lean mass. 3–5 g a day. Early signal for cognition.
A
Omega-3, D, Mg
No protection against heart disease or cancer in a 25,000-person trial. Correct a deficiency, then stop.
A
Caffeine
Reliably improves alertness. Half-life about five hours, so before noon.
A
Nootropics
Beyond caffeine, little human data in healthy adults.
C
Lowe et al., JAMA Intern Med 2020 · Liu et al., NEJM 2022 · Manson et al. (VITAL), NEJM 2019 · ISSN 2017

Career sustainability
Your capacity at sixty is built in your forties.
A career of forty years is an endurance event. Train for decades, not quarters.

The time audit
4 of 168 h
The minimum effective week costs about four hours.
2 × 45 min strength plus 150 min brisk activity. Sleep is not extra time; it is time you protect.
