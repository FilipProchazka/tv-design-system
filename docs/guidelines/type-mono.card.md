Geist Mono for anything that is a fact rather than narration: kickers, meta, units, citations, prices, axes, evidence grades, tabular columns. 500 for the tracked uppercase kicker, 400 for data; no other mono weight.

Figures at display size are Geist, not mono: 400 on the web layer, 300 on a board at 100px and up. Mono keeps the unit and the label beside the figure. Mono never sets a sentence.
