**PLOT** · 59 · Forest plot

**Use when**
Several effect estimates and their intervals.

**Roles**
- claim — 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- data — mono, tabular

**Rules**
- Null line drawn and labelled; effect direction named in mono at both ends.
- Rows ordered by the same logic as the argument, not by size.
- No gridlines, no legend box: label the series at the end of the line or on the bar.
- Axis labels, units and values are mono with tabular numerals. Never red against green.
