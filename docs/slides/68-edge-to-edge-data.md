**Use when**
A curve as atmosphere behind a claim.

**Roles**
- claim — 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- body — evidence, 32px floor
- label — mono, tracked caps

**Rules**
- Decoration-grade only: no axis, no values, and never cite it.
- If the numbers matter, use 60 Time series.
- No gridlines, no legend box: label the series at the end of the line or on the bar.
- Axis labels, units and values are mono with tabular numerals. Never red against green.
