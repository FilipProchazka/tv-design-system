**BESIDE** · 04 · Section divider

**Use when**
Entering a new part of the talk.

**Roles**
- statement — 102px, 3 lines
- lead — one supporting paragraph
- body — evidence, 32px floor
- label — mono, tracked caps
- data — mono, tabular

**Rules**
- One line of what the part covers, nothing else.
- Dark boards are punctuation: two or three per deck.
- The claim steps down to 58px in a BESIDE column; the column never widens.
- One idea beside one piece of evidence. Two pieces of evidence is two boards.
