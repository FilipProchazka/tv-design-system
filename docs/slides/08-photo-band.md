**BAND** · 08 · Photo band

**Use when**
The picture is the evidence, and needs the whole width.

**Roles**
- claim — 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- body — evidence, 32px floor
- photograph — duotone in Public mode

**Rules**
- One sentence under the band. If it needs two, the picture is not carrying it.
- Caption in mono says what we are looking at.
- Photographs in a band share one crop ratio and one duotone. Mixed crops read as a moodboard.
- The sentence under the band says what the pictures have in common.
