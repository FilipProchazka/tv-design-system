**SPLIT** · 67 · Two-phase split

**Use when**
Two phases of one topic compared.

**Roles**
- claim — 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- body — evidence, 32px floor
- label — mono, tracked caps

**Rules**
- One theme; the second half is one tint step darker. Never two topic colours.
- Colour never carries the distinction alone — the phase is named in text.
- Text column starts at x 120, the photograph at x 1200. Nothing invents another x.
- If the photograph is not the evidence, drop it and use OPEN.
