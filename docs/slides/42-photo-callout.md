**SPLIT** · 42 · Photo callout

**Use when**
Parts of one object, named.

**Roles**
- claim — 2 lines, ≤70 chars
- body — evidence, 32px floor
- data — mono, tabular
- photograph — duotone in Public mode

**Rules**
- Numbered callouts on the photograph, matching list in the left column.
- Duotone the photograph; a natural-colour flat-lay belongs to Clinical mode only.
- Text column starts at x 120, the photograph at x 1200. Nothing invents another x.
- If the photograph is not the evidence, drop it and use OPEN.
