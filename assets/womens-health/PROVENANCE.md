# Women's health library

Imported 22 Sep 2026 from the project "Women's Health – vitalita v longevity medicíně" (deck, print handout, online handout). Violet theme.

## Folders

- `photo/`: 17 originals, untreated.
- `duo/`: violet duotones. `cv-*` are the deck crops; `photo-*` are duotones of the originals.
- `flat/`: the deck's flat violet tints, used under the photo scrims.
- `portions/`: hand-portion guides (palm, fist, cupped hand, thumb) in neutral and violet. See its own PROVENANCE.md.
- `handout/`: the crops and hand guides used by the online handout.

## Use

Subject photography, fits the imagery rule: photo-archive, photo-bar, photo-chart, photo-kitchen, photo-lift, photo-rest, photo-squash, photo-steth, photo-walk, photo-midlife, and the cv-lift, cv-strength, cv-sleep, cv-plate, cv-fruit, cv-food, cv-shake-wide, cv-part3, cv-part5, cv-thanks crops.

## Public talks only, never under her name

Stock portraits and lifestyle styling. Allowed on public talks where the topic is a person's experience; never on a cover, speaker or closing board that carries her name or affiliations: photo-cover, photo-gym, photo-highkey, photo-clinic, photo-portrait, and the cv-cover, cv-part1, cv-part2, cv-close crops. photo-food is a flat-lay. photo-organs is an illustration, not a photograph.
