# womens-health

The images of the women's health and longevity talk (Ženské zdraví, violet theme, September 2026) and of its two handouts. Added in the 26 September 2026 sync from the repository's `templates/` folders; the templates themselves did not come across, so this group is the only place to take these files from.

## What is here

| folder | what | use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `duo/cv-*.jpg` | the talk's photographs with a violet wash | the set to use on violet pieces |
| `flat/cv-*.jpg` | the same photographs, a second rendering with a violet wash | a duplicate set; prefer `duo/` |
| `duo/photo-*.jpg` | the deck `photo` library re-washed violet | use the `photo` group instead unless the piece is violet |
| `photo/photo-*.jpg` | a copy of the deck `photo` library in natural colour | a duplicate of the `photo` group; take those from `photo` |
| `portions/hand-*-violet.png` | the four hand-portion marks (cupped, fist, palm, thumb) cut out, violet ink | the portion marks for violet pieces and the handout |
| `portions/hand-*.webp` | the same marks, uncoloured | the source to re-ink for another theme |
| `handout/*` | the online handout's images: cover, plate, fruit, shake, sleep, strength and the four hands in handout size | the online handout only |
| `handout/qr-dropbox.png` | the handout's QR code | the handout only; check the link still resolves before reuse |

## The portrait

`duo/cv-tereza.jpg` and `flat/cv-tereza.jpg` are the one real photograph of her in the system: the podcast-studio shot, both copies violet-washed. It goes on the Speaker layout and the CV, nowhere else. On a blue or petrol board re-grade it to that theme's transfer curve first (`guidelines/IMAGERY.md`); the wash here is not the measured duotone.

`photo/photo-portrait.jpg` is a stock stand-in, not her. It does not ship anywhere, as `assets/img/PROVENANCE.md` says of the whole `photo` set.

## Rights

No provenance is recorded for this group. The `photo-*` files are the deck library, whose sources `assets/img/PROVENANCE.md` traces to her own source deck; the `cv-*` files, the hands and the handout images came with the women's health talk and carry no record. As with the `clinical` group: internal use until the sources and the portrait's photographer are confirmed, and nothing from here on the public site before that.

## Open

Which rendering stays (`duo/` or `flat/`), and whether the duplicated `photo/` set and the WebP hands stay here or are removed, is her decision; until then use what the table says.
